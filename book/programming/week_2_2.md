# Programming with large matrices

This chapter contains a information that is aimed at improving your programming skills, in relation to dealing with setting up and solving large systems of equations. A selection of python tools is introduced that are relevant for PDE simulation methods, but that each have wider applicabiility. The central objective is computational efficiency. Sometimes a positive side-effect of using the right tools is improved readability of the code, although at other times readability needs to be sacrificed in favor of runtime.
