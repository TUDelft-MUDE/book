# Introduction to Reliability

As we previously introduced, _Reliability_ is the probability of a device or system performing its intended function under given conditions for a specified period of time. In this section, we will introduced two perspectives for performing reliability assessments:
1. Defining a limit state function and computing the probability of being in such failure region;
2. Analyzing the failure distributions from a survivability perspective.
