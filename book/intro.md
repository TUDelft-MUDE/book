# Welcome to the MUDE Textbook

Welcome to the MUDE textbook for the 2026-27 academic year. This is where assigned reading is located, along with interactive exercises to practice and study the module material. Material is released according to the course schedule.

The most recent complete book is the 2025-26 book, which is avaiable [here](https://mude.citg.tudelft.nl/book/2025).

## Spot a mistake?

If you spot a mistake, you can click on {octicon}`mark-github`, login with your github account and report your issue or suggest an edit. It’ll be solved soon!

## Have a question / answer?

If you’ve a question regarding any content, look at [https://​answers​.citg​.tudelft​.nl/](https://answers.citg.tudelft.nl/) to see if it’s already there. If not, ask a new question! Your teachers and TAs will look into it soon! If you’ve an answer to on of the question posted there, you’re welcome to answer questions yourself!
